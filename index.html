Hear what the engineer hears

ACA reads your physics every frame and compares your inputs against the circuit’s native boundaries. A small in-game app streams data over a UDP bridge to a cloud engine that works out the one thing worth saying—then says it, before the next braking zone.

  • In-game Python app samples speed, pedals, and position.
  • UDP bridge hands processing off, so your frame rate stays untouched.
  • Cloud engine returns a short spoken call to your headset.
