Before you start

  • Assetto Corsa + Content Manager

    The original Assetto Corsa (not Competizione or EVO), with Content Manager installed.

  • Headset or speakers

    Radio calls play through your default audio output. A headset works best.

  • Internet connection

    Coaching is generated in the cloud, so you need to be online while you drive.

Install in four steps

No launcher, no config files, no per-track setup.

Step 1

Download the package

Grab ACA.zip using the button above. Leave it zipped. Content Manager reads the archive directly.

Step 2

Drag it into Content Manager

Open Content Manager and drop the zip anywhere on its window. When it asks to install the app, confirm.

Step 3

Enable ACA in-game

Start any session. Open the app bar on the edge of the screen and switch on ACA from the list.

Step 4

Drive

Pick any car and any track and run a few laps. Your engineer starts calling out corrections as you get on the throttle.

What you should hear

Short, specific calls, one per corner at most. If something sounds wrong, vague, or badly timed, that’s exactly the feedback we need.

Something not working?

ACA doesn’t show up in the in-game app list

Close and relaunch Assetto Corsa after installing, then check the app bar again. If it’s still missing, reinstall by dragging the zip into Content Manager a second time.

I can’t hear the engineer

Calls play through your default Windows audio device. Make sure your headset is set as the default before launching the game, and that you’re connected to the internet.

My frame rate dropped

It shouldn’t. ACA is built to leave your FPS alone. If you see a drop, that’s a bug worth reporting. Include your car, track, and PC specs.
